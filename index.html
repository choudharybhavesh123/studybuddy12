STEP-BY-STEP WORKBOOK PREVIEW
"Q: A 1,200 kg car accelerates from 0 to 20 m/s in 8 seconds. Find the force."
Step 1: a = (20 - 0) / 8 = 2.5 m/s²
Step 2: F = m × a = 1,200 × 2.5 = 3,000 N
💡 Why it works: Force is just the physical push required to change velocity!
